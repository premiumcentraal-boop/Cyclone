# Cyclone 3.8.5

Cyclone 3.8.5 replaces the old card-style accessibility overlay with a compact, animated AI mode inspired by a bottom-edge aurora.

## What changed

- A mostly transparent Cyclone orb replaces the always-visible `Ask Cyclone` pill.
- Triple-tap the orb, or press and hold it for three seconds, to open AI mode.
- The activation treatment uses a rotating rainbow ring before the cyan, blue, violet and magenta aurora rises from the bottom edge.
- AI mode keeps Pause/Resume on the left and adds Minimize and Exit controls on the right.
- The composer now accepts typed requests and runs them through the existing `OpenRouterAdaptiveAgent` path.
- The microphone button performs Android speech recognition after an explicit, reversible microphone grant.
- Working, Live, GATE and Done remain within the same semi-transparent aurora instead of switching to opaque floating cards.
- Done can always be dismissed and also returns to the orb automatically after 5.5 seconds.
- Closing GATE cancels the AI run and never confirms or completes the consequential action.
- Ordinary host observations no longer collect the full animated overlay tree, preserving host controls such as named Clock tabs; GATE copy remains observable.
